# V8.6 execution plan — audio, motion, symmetry

## Observed problems

- A moving pixel can visibly advance, reverse rapidly, then continue.
- Junction crossing can feel like a tiny stop/restart.
- Music detection misses subtle spectral peaks and textures.
- Symmetric compositions can contain unrelated asymmetric secondary content.
- Lightning/pathway is too local and changes too quickly.
- Some structural blocks are not constructed cleanly enough.

## Confirmed code causes

1. V8.5 collision logic can multiply `direction` by `-1` while the head is in the middle of an edge.
2. Junction crossing clamps progress to 0/1 and drops sub-frame overshoot.
3. Music brain mainly relies on broad RMS/transient channels even though the full spectrum is available.
4. Secondary structure branches and retained feedback are not always symmetry-safe.
5. Lightning endpoints are chosen locally and recycled too frequently.

## V8.6 changes

- Never reverse a mobile head in the middle of an edge.
- Preserve progress overshoot into the next segment.
- Add 9 adaptive spectral bands, novelty, flux, centroid, spread, entropy, crest and rolloff.
- Keep global volume as silence/context, not global brightness.
- Make symmetry binary and clear incompatible retained state when entering mirrored grammar.
- Give lightning endpoints on opposite sides of the graph and slow its head over multiple beats/bars.

## Invariants

- Never alter the physical `endGrid` topology.
- Pixels stay physically fixed.
- Maximum three coherent colors.
- Mobile heads remain autonomous rather than flocking.
- V8.5 is the rollback baseline.

## Validation

Use `docs/TEST_PROTOCOL.md`. Do not merge this branch until TouchDesigner runtime test reports empty warnings/errors and the user approves a short video.
