## Review discipline

A low score is not a request to rewrite unrelated scenes.

Revise only the scene under review unless the critique explicitly identifies a shared renderer/semantic problem.

When a scene reaches `approved`, preserve its accepted behaviour and create a new version if later experiments are needed.
